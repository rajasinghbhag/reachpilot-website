Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ReachPilot handles information when users connect their Google accounts, manage email campaigns, send emails, and use optional unsubscribe and suppression features.

Desktop application Local data storage Google OAuth Gmail API

Effective date: November 23, 2026

1. Overview

ReachPilot is a desktop email management application that allows users to manage email campaigns and send emails through their authorized Google Gmail accounts using the Gmail API.

ReachPilot is designed to store its primary application data locally on the user's own computer. ReachPilot does not operate a central cloud database or server for storing users' campaign information, recipient lists, email content, or application settings.

2. Information Handled by ReachPilot

Depending on the features used, ReachPilot may handle:

  • Google Account Information: The Google account email address associated with an authorized Gmail account, when available.
  • OAuth Credentials: Authorization tokens required to connect ReachPilot to Google services.
  • Email Information: Recipient email addresses, subject lines, message content, and other information entered or imported by the user for sending emails.
  • Campaign Information: Campaign configurations, recipient selections, and related application settings.
  • Suppression Information: Unsubscribe-related email addresses, dates, and statuses used to prevent further emails to unsubscribed recipients.
  • Operational Information: Application settings and operational status or error information generated during use.

3. Google API Access and Data Usage

ReachPilot requests the following Google OAuth scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send

This permission is used to send email messages configured and initiated by the user through ReachPilot.

The Gmail sending functionality does not require reading the user's Gmail inbox or accessing unrelated Gmail information.

Google account authorization is handled through Google's OAuth authorization process. OAuth credentials are used only to provide the Google functionality requested by the user.

ReachPilot does not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or use it for purposes unrelated to providing the application's functionality.

ReachPilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Local Data Storage and Google Sheets Integration

4.1 Local Application Storage

ReachPilot stores its primary application data locally on the user's own computer.

This includes:

  • Campaign information and configurations.
  • Recipient lists and email content.
  • Application settings and account configurations.
  • Locally stored suppression records.
  • OAuth credential files used for Google authorization.

ReachPilot does not operate its own central cloud storage server or database to collect and store this primary application data. The application does not automatically upload this information to ReachPilot-operated servers.

4.2 Optional Google Sheets Suppression Integration

ReachPilot provides an optional Google Sheets integration for managing unsubscribe and email suppression records.

When a recipient uses the unsubscribe link included in an email sent through ReachPilot, the link opens a Google Apps Script web application. The recipient's email address is passed to this service so that the recipient can confirm the unsubscribe request.

After confirmation, the Google Apps Script records the recipient's email address, unsubscribe date, and unsubscribe status in the associated Google Sheet.

ReachPilot can retrieve suppression records from the Google Sheet selected by the user through its CSV export interface. Retrieved records are stored locally and used to prevent further emails from being sent to suppressed addresses.

The desktop application's CSV synchronization retrieves information from the selected Google Sheet. It does not upload campaign information, general recipient lists, or email content to that Sheet through this synchronization process.

Information recorded in Google Sheets and processed through Google Apps Script is subject to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

ReachPilot does not sell Google user data or disclose it to third parties for advertising or unrelated commercial purposes.

Information is transmitted to Google services only when necessary to provide functionality requested by the user, including Gmail email sending and optional Google Sheets unsubscribe-record processing.

Email messages sent through Gmail are processed by Google according to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

When a recipient uses an unsubscribe link, the recipient's email address is transmitted to the Google Apps Script web application configured for ReachPilot. Following the recipient's confirmation, the address, unsubscribe date, and unsubscribe status are recorded in the associated Google Sheet.

This processing is used to maintain unsubscribe records and prevent further emails from being sent to unsubscribed recipients.

ReachPilot does not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

6. Data Security and Protection Mechanisms

ReachPilot uses a local-storage architecture to reduce unnecessary transfer and centralized storage of user information. The following measures describe how data is stored and protected.

6.1 Local Storage and Access Control

Primary application data and OAuth credential files are stored on the user's own computer rather than on a central ReachPilot-operated server.

Access to these local files is subject to the operating system's user-account permissions and the security configuration of the computer. Users should protect their computer with a strong login password and prevent unauthorized access to their user account.

6.2 OAuth Credential Protection

OAuth authorization tokens are stored locally on the computer running ReachPilot and are used to authorize the Google functionality requested by the user.

OAuth credential files are stored as local application files. ReachPilot does not claim that these files are encrypted at rest. Users should not share OAuth token files or application data folders with unauthorized individuals.

If a user suspects unauthorized access to their Google authorization credentials, they can revoke ReachPilot's access through their Google Account security settings.

6.3 Secure Data Transmission

ReachPilot communicates with Google OAuth, Gmail API, and Google Apps Script services through their HTTPS endpoints. SMTP email transmission uses STARTTLS where supported by the configured SMTP service.

Google Sheets suppression data is retrieved using the URL configured by the user. Users should configure a secure HTTPS Google Sheets URL and use a trusted network connection when synchronizing suppression information.

6.4 Data Minimization

ReachPilot requests only the Gmail permission required for its email-sending functionality. It does not require Gmail inbox-reading permission for this purpose.

Optional Google Sheets integration is limited to unsubscribe and suppression information required for recipient suppression.

6.5 Device Security Responsibilities

Since primary application information is stored locally, protection of that information also depends on the user's device security.

Users are encouraged to keep their operating system updated, use appropriate device security protections, restrict access to authorized users, and maintain secure backups where necessary.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ReachPilot does not claim that local storage alone eliminates every possible security risk.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Primary application information is retained locally on the user's computer for as long as it is needed to operate the application and perform the functions requested by the user.

Users can delete campaigns, recipient lists, templates, account configurations, and suppression records using the relevant deletion controls available in ReachPilot.

Removing a Gmail account or account set from ReachPilot does not necessarily delete its separately stored OAuth credential file. Users can manually delete these credential files from the local application data folder.

Users can revoke ReachPilot's Google account authorization through their Google Account security settings.

Unsubscribe information stored in the associated Google Sheet can be managed or deleted separately by the user from that Sheet. Such information remains in the Sheet until the user deletes it.

Separately created exports and backups may need to be deleted independently.

8. Children's Privacy

ReachPilot is not designed for children under the age of 13, and the application is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ReachPilot's functionality or data-handling practices change.

The effective date will be updated whenever material changes are made to this policy.
