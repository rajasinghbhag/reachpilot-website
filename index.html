Desktop email workflow

Organize your Gmail sending workflow with ReachPilot.

ReachPilot is a desktop application designed to help its operator manage authorized Gmail accounts and send email through Google's Gmail API. Users remain responsible for recipient consent and lawful email practices.

Desktop application Google OAuth Gmail API sending

Manage connected accounts

Authorize Gmail accounts through Google's consent flow and manage the accounts configured in the app.

Send email

Send messages through authorized Gmail accounts. ReachPilot does not bypass Gmail limits or anti-abuse rules.

Responsible email practices

Use appropriate recipient consent, accurate sender information, and honor applicable unsubscribe and opt-out requests.

Recipient consent: ReachPilot must not be used to send unsolicited commercial emails or spam. Users are responsible for ensuring that recipients have appropriate permission to receive messages.

Unsubscribe and opt-out

Recipients must be able to opt out of future emails wherever required. Unsubscribe requests should be respected and suppressed from future campaigns.

Google user data notice

ReachPilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including its Limited Use requirements.
